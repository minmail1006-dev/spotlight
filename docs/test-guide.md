# User test guide

## Before the session (facilitator)
1. Log in as the admin account and open **Me → Admin & test tools**.
2. Press **Reset plans (keep event log)** so everyone starts with the same 10 sample plans.
3. Keep the **venue codes** table open. You play the venue staff and give out codes.
4. Give each tester a different test account (Alex, Sam, Robin, Kim, Charlie, Jamie) or let them create their own.

## Tasks to give testers (don't help unless they are stuck > 1 min)
1. "Find something to do this evening and join it."
2. "Post a plan for watching football tomorrow at a sports bar."
3. Pair two testers on the same plan. "You have both arrived at the venue. Check in." (Give the code when asked.)
4. "Show me the discount you would show to the staff."
5. "Something about a plan feels wrong. What would you do?"

The sample plan **"Catan & chill, beginners welcome"** at Dice & Dram is always "happening now"
after a reset, so check-in can be tested immediately.

## Assumptions to test (state these in the report)
- Will people post a plan publicly, and will they join a plan with strangers?
- Does the 2-person rule feel fair or frustrating?
- Can a user complete the whole flow unaided, and how long does it take? (Admin page: median time)
- Would a venue accept 15–20 %? (Ask 2–3 real venue owners separately.)

## After the session
1. Admin page → **Download event log (CSV)**.
2. Write down what you observed and what you changed (e.g. lower discount, off-peak limits, other threshold).
3. Be honest that classmates are not the real target group.

## Questions to ask afterwards
- What would stop you from joining a plan with people you don't know?
- Did the discount matter, or was the plan itself the reason to go?
- Was anything confusing? Where did you hesitate?
